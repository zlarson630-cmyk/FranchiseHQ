# Illinois Basketball — Roster Construction Rules

Applies to the College Hoops 2K8 Illinois dynasty in Franchise HQ.

## Roster planning structure
- Construct the roster around **Guard (three deep), Wing (three deep), and Big (three deep)** rather than five required on-court positions.
- Group ranks are roster-planning priorities, **not** a required starting five or a playing-time promise.
- Players can be eligible for more than one planning group (primary and secondary roles), but a player must not occupy two slots in the same season.
- Actual lineup combinations are matchup-dependent and will be planned separately.

## Two essential roster capabilities
1. **True Point Guard (every-game requirement):** Passing, ball handling and decision-making, with adequate perimeter defense to remain playable. Prefer a top-three guard; the specialist need still exists if nobody qualifies.
2. **Post Stopper (matchup-dependent insurance):** Strong one-on-one interior defense against a dangerous post scorer. Need a rostered option, not necessarily a regular starter or rotation player. Deploy chiefly against teams with a major post scoring threat.

## Specialist grading
Grades assess the **capability**, not overall player talent or the best relative to the current roster:
- **A**: elite and a genuine advantage
- **B**: strong/dependable, meets the roster requirement
- **C**: functional, does **not** meet the specialist requirement
- **D/F**: weak fit or liability
- **Not evaluated**: no supported assessment yet; not equivalent to C or failure

Track all graded players, not only the roster leader. A season has a **confirmed capability** only when at least one eligible player is graded **B or better**. An unassessed position is **unverified**, not automatically a vacancy.
